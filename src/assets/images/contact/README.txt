CONTACT PAGE IMAGES — HOW TO ADD
================================

Save the images directly in THIS folder (src/assets/images/contact/), using
exactly these file names (.webp, .jpg, .jpeg, .png or .avif). Save, refresh the
browser, and the placeholder is replaced automatically.

Free sources: unsplash.com, pexels.com, pixabay.com
Keep each file under ~300 KB (convert to WebP at squoosh.app).

  contact-header-bg   1920 x 700    dark office / city skyline at dusk (header background;
                                    a dark overlay is added in code)
  contact-office      1200 x 800    your real office or team photo
  contact-map         1400 x 600    screenshot of your location from Google Maps
                                    (Maps > Share > screenshot), or any map image

Also update your real phone, email, address and hours in
src/components/Contact/contactData.js
